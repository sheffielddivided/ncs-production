News feeds

Saved in data/company_feeds.json and shared by everyone · fetched daily and right after saving
Paste the address of the company's RSS or Atom feed. A normal news page works too, if it advertises its feed — the fetch job looks for it. For companies listed on Oslo Børs, a search on NewsWeb filtered on the company also works: paste the address of the search page. Some news pages without a feed can also be read directly (currently pages built like ORLEN's, Harbour Energy's and Shell Norge's news lists). Feeds are read by a GitHub Actions job, not by your browser, because most sites block cross-site reading. Saving writes the list to the repository with your GitHub token (the same as for hub settings); the job then fetches the new feed right away (usually within a minute — the status below updates by itself) and all feeds every morning. Feeds apply to the whole corporate group.
